# Billing FAQ

## Understanding Your Bill

**Q: Why was I charged more than expected?**
Common reasons:
- Plan upgrade mid-cycle (prorated charge)
- Regional sales tax applied
- Currency conversion fee from your card issuer (for international customers)
- Add-on feature activated

**Q: Where can I see my invoices?**
Account Settings → Billing → Invoice History. Invoices are generated on your billing date and available as PDF.

**Q: What does "Pending" mean on my charge?**
Authorization holds appear as "Pending" for 1–5 business days before becoming confirmed charges. This is normal.

## Payment Issues

**Q: My payment failed — what happens now?**
1. We'll retry the charge automatically after 3 days, then again after 7 days
2. You'll receive an email each time a retry fails
3. After 3 failed attempts, your account will be locked pending payment update
4. Update your payment method in Account Settings → Billing → Payment Methods

**Q: I see a charge I don't recognize. What should I do?**
1. Check if a family member or colleague might have used your account
2. Check our invoice for line items — sometimes add-ons appear as separate charges
3. If genuinely unrecognized, submit a billing dispute ticket
4. For confirmed fraud, contact your card issuer directly and notify us

**Q: Can I get a refund for a billing error?**
Yes. Documented billing errors (duplicate charges, wrong plan billed, etc.) are refunded within 5–7 business days.

## Payment Methods

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
See our Payment Methods document for the full list. Generally: Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, PayPal, and bank transfer.

**Q: Can I pay annually to save money?**
Yes. Annual billing saves 20% vs monthly. Switch in Account Settings → Subscription → Billing Cycle.

**Q: How do I update my payment method?**
Account Settings → Billing → Payment Methods → Add New. You can set a new card as default without deleting the old one.

## Subscription Billing

**Q: When am I billed?**
On the same day each month/year as your original subscription date. E.g., if you subscribed on the 15th, you're billed on the 15th.

**Q: What happens if my billing date falls on a weekend or holiday?**
The charge is attempted on the next business day.

**Q: I cancelled my subscription. Will I be charged again?**
No — cancellation stops future renewals immediately. You retain access until the end of your current paid period.

**Q: I was charged after cancelling. What happened?**
This sometimes occurs if cancellation happened within 24 hours of your billing date. Submit a ticket with your cancellation confirmation email — we'll refund within 48 hours if confirmed.
