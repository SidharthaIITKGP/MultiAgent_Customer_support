# Account Troubleshooting

## Can't Log In

### Wrong Password
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a reset link
4. Link expires in 60 minutes — request a new one if it expires
5. If you reset your password successfully but still can't log in, clear your browser cookies and try again

### Account Locked
Your account may be locked for these reasons:
- **5+ failed login attempts** in 30 minutes (auto-lock, 1 hour duration)
- **Suspicious login activity** detected from an unrecognized location
- **Failed payment** on a subscription
- **Manual hold** by our security or trust & safety team

To unlock:
- Auto-locked accounts unlock automatically after 1 hour
- Payment-related locks: update your payment method in Account Settings
- For security-related locks: submit a ticket — identity verification required
- **Password reset is always available even when account is locked**

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Issues
- If you've lost access to your 2FA device, use a backup code (saved during 2FA setup)
- No backup codes? Submit a ticket with government-issued ID to verify your identity
- Recovery typically takes 24–48 hours for security reasons

### Email Address Changed / No Longer Accessible
Submit a ticket with:
1. Your username or account ID
2. Last 4 digits of the payment method on file
3. Approximate date of account creation

## Account Management

### How to Change Your Email
1. Log in → Account Settings → Personal Information
2. Click "Change Email"
3. Enter new email and confirm with your password
4. Verify via link sent to the NEW email address

### How to Change Your Password (when logged in)
1. Account Settings → Security → Change Password
2. Enter current password, then new password twice
3. Minimum requirements: 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character

### How to Enable/Disable 2FA
- Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication
- Recommended: Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) over SMS

### Suspicious Activity on Your Account
If you notice unfamiliar logins or changes:
1. Change your password immediately
2. Enable 2FA if not already enabled
3. Submit a ticket marked "Urgent — Account Compromise"
4. Our security team will audit your account within 2 hours

## Browser & App Issues
- **Recommended browsers**: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Clear cache/cookies** if experiencing display or login issues
- **Disable extensions**: Ad blockers can sometimes interfere with login flows
- **Mobile app issues**: Force-close the app, clear app cache in device settings, then reopen
