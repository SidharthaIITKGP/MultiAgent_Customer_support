# Shipping Policy

## Processing Times
- **Standard orders**: 1–2 business days to process before shipping
- **Express orders**: Same-day processing if ordered before 12 PM EST
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day

## Delivery Timeframes
| Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 5–8 business days | $4.99 |
| Express Shipping | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |
| Free Threshold | 5–8 business days | Free on orders $50+ |

## International Shipping
- Available to 45+ countries
- Delivery: 7–21 business days depending on destination
- Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility
- We are not liable for delays caused by customs processing

## Order Tracking
- Tracking number emailed within 24 hours of shipment
- Track at: account portal → Orders → Track
- Carrier: USPS, UPS, or FedEx depending on destination and method

## What If My Order Is Late?
- Contact us if your order hasn't arrived 3 business days after estimated delivery
- For orders over 15 business days past estimated delivery with no tracking update, we'll initiate a replacement or refund investigation

## Address Changes
- Address changes accepted within 1 hour of order placement
- After that, we cannot redirect orders in transit — wait for delivery or refusal then contact us

## Undeliverable Packages
- If a package is returned as undeliverable, we'll contact you to arrange re-shipment (re-shipping fees apply)
- If we cannot reach you within 14 days, the order will be refunded minus shipping costs

## Lost or Stolen Packages
- If tracking shows "delivered" but you didn't receive it:
  1. Wait 48 hours (carrier delays are common)
  2. Check with neighbors / building management
  3. File a claim with us — we'll investigate with the carrier
  4. Resolution typically within 5–7 business days
