# Escalation Policy — When to Expect a Human Agent

## Automatic Escalation Triggers
The following situations are automatically routed to a senior human agent:

1. **Account compromise** — Suspected unauthorized access, unusual login locations
2. **Legal / regulatory requests** — GDPR data requests, subpoenas, law enforcement inquiries
3. **High-value disputes** — Transactions over $500 in dispute
4. **Repeated unresolved issues** — Same issue reported 3+ times without resolution
5. **Explicit escalation request** — Customer explicitly asks for a human or supervisor
6. **Critical urgency tickets** — Marked as Critical, e.g., service disruption affecting business operations
7. **Angry or distressed customers** — Sentiment analysis detects high frustration or distress
8. **Failed automated actions** — If automated tools (refund, password reset) fail due to system errors

## Escalation Tiers

### Tier 1: Standard Support Agent
- Handles routine tickets (refunds, basic troubleshooting, account questions)
- Average resolution: 4–48 hours depending on plan

### Tier 2: Senior Support Specialist
- Complex technical issues, billing disputes over $100, escalated Tier 1 cases
- Access to advanced diagnostic tools and account modification capabilities
- Average resolution: 24–72 hours

### Tier 3: Engineering / Trust & Safety
- Bugs requiring code fixes, legal holds, account security investigations
- Average resolution: 2–10 business days

## What to Expect After Escalation
1. You'll receive an email confirming escalation with a new ticket priority and estimated response time
2. A named agent will be assigned — you'll see their name in correspondence
3. You won't need to re-explain your issue — the escalation includes full ticket history
4. If you need to provide additional information, the agent will request it directly

## How to Request Escalation
In any open ticket, reply with: "I would like to escalate this ticket to a supervisor."
Your ticket will be escalated within 1 hour during business hours.

## Escalation Commitment
- We do not close escalated tickets without customer confirmation
- If escalation resolution is unsatisfactory, you may request a second review once
- All escalation decisions are logged and reviewed monthly for quality assurance

## When We CANNOT Escalate Further
- Decisions made by our legal team regarding regulatory compliance
- Account closures due to Terms of Service violations (after appeals process is exhausted)
- Final billing decisions confirmed by Finance

## After Escalation Resolution
- We send a satisfaction survey — your feedback directly impacts agent training
- Resolved escalation cases remain open for 7 days in case you have follow-up questions
