# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: How do I contact customer support?**
You can reach us via live chat (available 9 AM – 9 PM EST), email at support@example.com, or by submitting a ticket through this portal. Average response time is under 4 hours during business hours.

**Q: What are your business hours?**
Our support team is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM to 9 PM EST. Weekend support is available for Critical-priority tickets only.

**Q: How do I track my ticket?**
After submitting a ticket, you'll receive an email confirmation with a ticket ID. You can check status at any time in your account portal under "Support History."

**Q: Can I update my ticket after submitting it?**
Yes. Reply to the email confirmation or log into the portal and add a comment to your open ticket.

**Q: What's the difference between Basic and Premium plans?**
- **Basic**: Email support only, 48-hour response SLA, standard features
- **Premium**: Live chat + email, 4-hour response SLA, priority queue, dedicated account manager for enterprise

**Q: How do I upgrade my plan?**
Go to Account Settings → Subscription → Upgrade. Changes take effect immediately and you'll be charged a prorated amount.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I'm unhappy with the service?**
Yes — see our Refund Policy document for full details. Generally, you have 30 days from purchase to request a refund on digital products.

**Q: My account is showing "locked" — what does that mean?**
A locked account usually means a failed payment, suspicious login attempts, or a manual hold by our security team. Submit a ticket and include your user ID; our team can unlock it after verification. You can still initiate a password reset even while your account is locked.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
Go to Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. Note: this is irreversible. All data will be purged within 30 days per our Privacy Policy.

**Q: I never received my confirmation email. What should I do?**
Check your spam/junk folder. Add support@example.com to your safe-senders list. If still not received within 30 minutes, contact us and we'll resend manually.
