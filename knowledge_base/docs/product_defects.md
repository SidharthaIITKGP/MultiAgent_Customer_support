# Product Defects Policy

## What Counts as a Defect?
- Item arrived broken, cracked, or physically damaged
- Item does not function as described in product listing
- Item is missing components listed in the product description
- Item received is different from what was ordered (wrong item shipped)
- Manufacturing defect discovered within 90 days of delivery

## What Does NOT Count as a Defect?
- Cosmetic wear from normal use
- Damage caused by misuse, drops, or unauthorized modifications
- Incompatibility with your specific device/software version (check compatibility list before purchasing)
- Consumable parts (batteries, filters) that have degraded with normal use

## Defect Window
- **Physical goods**: 90 days from delivery date
- **Software/digital products**: 30 days from purchase (bugs reported to our technical team)
- **Warranty items**: As specified in the product warranty document

## How to Report a Defect

### Step 1: Document the issue
Take photos or video clearly showing the defect. For software, include screenshots and error codes.

### Step 2: Submit a ticket
- Category: Product Defect
- Include: Order ID, description of defect, photos/video, your contact preference

### Step 3: Review process
- Our team reviews within 24 hours (48 hours on weekends)
- We may request additional photos or a short video demonstrating the defect
- For items under $50: typically approved without return required
- For items over $50: return shipping label provided, inspection upon receipt

### Step 4: Resolution options
1. **Replacement**: Identical item shipped at no cost (2–5 business days)
2. **Full refund**: Processed within 3–7 business days
3. **Repair credit**: Store credit to cover repair cost (if item is repairable)
4. **Partial refund**: For minor defects that don't impact core functionality

## Shipping Defective Items Back
- We provide a prepaid return label for confirmed defective items
- Pack securely — additional damage during return may affect resolution
- Keep tracking number until refund/replacement is confirmed

## High-Volume Defect Reports (Product-Wide Issues)
If we become aware of a defect affecting multiple units, we will:
1. Post a notice on our status page
2. Proactively email affected customers
3. Offer replacement or refund without requiring a ticket submission

## Escalation
If your defect report is denied and you believe it was incorrectly assessed, you can escalate by replying to the resolution email with "Request Supervisor Review." A senior agent will re-evaluate within 48 hours.
