# Refund Policy

## Overview
We want you to be completely satisfied. If you're not happy with your purchase, we'll do our best to make it right.

## Eligibility Window
- **Digital products / subscriptions**: 30 days from purchase date
- **Physical goods**: 30 days from delivery confirmation date
- **Defective items**: 90 days from delivery, regardless of refund window

## Eligible Scenarios
1. Item not as described
2. Item damaged on arrival
3. Order never received (after 15 business days past estimated delivery)
4. Duplicate charge / billing error
5. Service outage exceeding 24 continuous hours (credit applied, not cash refund)
6. Change of mind — within 30-day window only

## Ineligible Scenarios
- Refund requested outside the applicable window
- Item already used and not defective
- Subscription cancelled mid-cycle (you retain access until end of billing period; no partial refund)
- Orders currently in transit (refund is evaluated after delivery)
- Refund already processed for the same order

## Refund Amount
- Full refund for eligible physical items returned in original condition
- Full refund for digital products within eligibility window
- Partial refund possible for partially used subscriptions (case-by-case basis)
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the error was ours

## Refund Timeline
- Credit/debit card: 3–7 business days after approval
- PayPal: 1–3 business days
- Bank transfer: 5–10 business days
- Store credit: Instant

## How to Request a Refund
1. Submit a support ticket with subject "Refund Request"
2. Include: Order ID, reason for refund, preferred refund method
3. Our agent will verify eligibility and initiate the refund
4. You'll receive a confirmation email with a Refund ID once approved

## Repeated Refund Requests
Accounts with more than 3 refund requests in a 6-month period may be flagged for review. Fraudulent refund claims will result in account suspension.

## Locked Accounts and Refunds
If your account is locked, refunds cannot be processed until the account is restored. Contact support to unlock your account first, then resubmit the refund request.
