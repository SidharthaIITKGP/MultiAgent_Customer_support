# Order Tracking Guide

## How to Track Your Order

### Via Account Portal
1. Log in → Orders → [Order ID]
2. View current status, tracking number, and estimated delivery date
3. Click "Track Package" to open carrier tracking page in a new tab

### Via Email
- Your shipping confirmation email contains a direct tracking link
- Update emails are sent at: Label Created, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered

### Via Carrier Website
| Carrier | Tracking URL |
|---|---|
| USPS | usps.com/track |
| UPS | ups.com/track |
| FedEx | fedex.com/tracking |

## Order Statuses Explained
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | Order received, preparing for shipment |
| Label Created | Shipping label generated; carrier hasn't picked up yet |
| In Transit | Package picked up and moving through carrier network |
| Out for Delivery | Package is on the delivery vehicle today |
| Delivered | Package marked as delivered by carrier |
| Delivery Attempted | Carrier tried to deliver but couldn't (no one home, access issue) |
| Return to Sender | Package being returned — contact us immediately |
| Exception | Unusual event (weather delay, lost in sort facility) — monitor closely |

## Estimated Delivery Times
- **Standard**: 5–8 business days after shipping date
- **Express**: 2–3 business days
- **Overnight**: Next business day (if shipped before 3 PM)
- Business days exclude weekends and US federal holidays

## Common Tracking Issues

### "Label Created" with no movement for 48+ hours
The carrier hasn't scanned your package yet. This sometimes happens during high-volume periods. If no movement after 72 hours, contact us.

### Tracking shows "Delivered" but I didn't receive it
1. Wait 48 hours — carrier scans sometimes precede actual delivery
2. Check with neighbors, mailroom, building management, or secondary delivery locations
3. Check if a notice was left for pickup at the post office
4. If still not found after 48 hours, submit a "Missing Delivered Package" ticket

### Tracking hasn't updated in several days
Tracking gaps of 2–3 days are normal for long-distance shipments. If no update for 7+ days, contact us.

### Wrong tracking number in my email
This is a known issue with multi-item orders. Contact us with your Order ID — we'll provide the correct tracking number directly.

## Delivery Instructions
- Add delivery instructions in checkout (leave at door, ring bell, etc.)
- Instructions are passed to the carrier but not guaranteed (carrier-dependent)
- For high-value items, consider requiring a signature in checkout

## International Tracking
- Tracking updates may be less frequent for international shipments
- Once the package enters the destination country, it may be handed to a local postal carrier
- Customs processing can add 3–10 business days without tracking updates
