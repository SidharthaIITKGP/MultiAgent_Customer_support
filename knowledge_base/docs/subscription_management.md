# Subscription Management

## Plan Overview
| Feature | Basic | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Support channel | Email only | Email + Live chat |
| Response SLA | 48 hours | 4 hours |
| Monthly price | $9.99/mo | $29.99/mo |
| Annual price | $95.90/yr (save 20%) | $287.90/yr (save 20%) |
| API access | Limited (1,000 calls/mo) | Full (50,000 calls/mo) |
| Data export | CSV only | CSV + JSON + API |
| Team seats | 1 | Up to 5 |

## Upgrading Your Plan

### From Basic to Premium
1. Account Settings → Subscription → Change Plan
2. Select Premium → Confirm
3. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle
4. Premium features activate immediately

### Annual vs Monthly Billing
- Switch at any time in Account Settings → Subscription → Billing Cycle
- Switching from monthly to annual: charged the annual rate immediately, monthly billing stops
- Switching from annual to monthly: takes effect at next renewal date (no mid-cycle downgrade)

## Downgrading Your Plan

### From Premium to Basic
- Downgrade takes effect at the **end of your current billing period**
- You retain Premium features until the period ends
- Data and settings are preserved; API limits are enforced after downgrade date
- No refund for the remaining Premium period

## Cancelling Your Subscription

### How to Cancel
1. Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel Subscription
2. Choose reason (optional but appreciated)
3. Confirm cancellation
4. You'll receive a confirmation email with your access end date

### What Happens After Cancellation
- Access continues until end of current paid period
- No further charges
- Your data is retained for 90 days, then scheduled for deletion
- You can reactivate within 90 days and all data will be restored

### Cancellation and Refunds
- Mid-cycle cancellations do not generate a refund (you retain access until period end)
- Exception: If you cancel within 24 hours of a renewal charge, request a refund via ticket

## Pausing Your Subscription
- Subscriptions can be paused for 1–3 months (Premium only)
- Pause in Account Settings → Subscription → Pause
- Billing is suspended during pause; features are suspended
- Subscription resumes automatically after the pause period

## Team / Multi-Seat Subscriptions
- Premium includes up to 5 seats
- Add seats: Account Settings → Team → Invite Member
- Remove seats: Click member → Remove from team (next renewal reflects new count)
- Each team member gets their own login; billing is consolidated to the account owner

## Subscription Renewal Issues
**If renewal fails:**
1. Check that your payment method is up to date
2. We retry automatically after 3 and 7 days
3. After 3 failed attempts, subscription is suspended (not cancelled)
4. Update payment method to reactivate immediately without losing data
